Discover Boho Club

A boutique hotel on Marbella's Golden Mile.

Close to Puerto Banús and the Mediterranean, Boho Club brings together intimate rooms and bungalows, destination dining, gardens, wellness and a social calendar in one private setting.


Check availability
Is Boho Club a five-star hotel?

Yes. Boho Club is an intimate five-star boutique hotel on Marbella's Golden Mile, with rooms and private bungalows set within landscaped gardens.

Can non-residents visit the restaurant?

Yes. Breakfast, lunch and dinner can be reserved by local residents and visitors who are not staying at the hotel.

How close is Boho Club to Puerto Banús?

Boho Club is located on Marbella's Golden Mile, conveniently positioned between central Marbella and Puerto Banús.

Can I book wellness without staying overnight?

Selected wellness treatments and experiences are available to non-residents, subject to availability.
